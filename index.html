The user

Amir is a freelance photographer. He hates chasing late payments, so he puts it off, and the money comes in later.

The problem

Chasing a client feels awkward, so freelancers wait. The team's first idea was automatic reminders that get firmer over time.

The

Two versions in one afternoon. Version A sends the reminders for you. Version B shows whether the client has opened the invoice. Switch between them.

Prototype · switch versions, tap an invoice
The conversation

In five test sessions, four people switched the reminders off again. They didn't want software being firm with their best client. Version B got a different reaction: knowing the invoice had been opened told them whether to wait or send a short note.

Cut: the escalating reminder sequence, an estimated three of work.

The call

Build the "opened" status first. It moves the leading directly: people act sooner when they know the client has seen the invoice. Look at reminders again next quarter.
